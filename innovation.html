Innovation

Research

CellScope is working on the innovation of automated weather forecasting

FRED shows what an automated weather desk can do today. We are working on what it can do next.

Forecasting has always needed people to watch, decide and explain. Much of that routine work, such as watching feeds, spotting what changed and telling it clearly, can run all day and all night without getting tired. That is the problem we are working on.

Our goal is automated forecasting that people can trust. That means a system that is fast, plain-spoken and clear about what it knows and does not.

Where we are pushing

Four areas of work

Automated coverage

Weather coverage that adapts to the sky: quiet when calm, focused when it is not, and always led by the most important hazard.

Trustworthy by default

Checks on every claim, fresh-data requirements and honest uncertainty, so the system refuses rather than guesses.

Forecast guidance

Early research into AI that learns where storms form and move. It is in training and not part of FRED yet.

Clear communication

Graphics, voice and video that put the hazard and the number first, for a broadcast, a phone or a Short.

What this is not

FRED does not replace the National Weather Service, local emergency managers or your own judgment. When there is a warning, follow the official one.

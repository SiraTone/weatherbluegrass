01 3D Storm Visualization

3D STORM VISUALIZATION38.96°N 98.00°W · 10 KFT TO 50 KFT · LEVEL-II VOLUME

Volumetric storm rendering with height-tagged structure. Red cores, gold anvils, reflectivity on a real dBZ scale. Built for severe weather breakdowns and case studies.

  • Still frames and turntable loops
  • Any storm, any time window

02 3D Weather Graphs

3D WEATHER GRAPHS38.96°N 98.00°W · SEP 26 TO SEP 28 · 6AM TO 2AM

Timeline graphs with the CS banner, peak callouts, and a perspective grid. You set the location, dates, units, colors, and logos. These generate automatically in CellScope Studio, so nothing needs hand editing.

  • Cloud cover: clear to overcast, peak 72%
  • Wind speed: calm to extreme, peak 16 mph
  • Gust meter: gust against sustained, peak 26 mph
  • Temperature, feels-like, dewpoint bar graphs

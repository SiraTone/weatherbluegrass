Separate class

03 AI models

Machine-learned models compete on a different footing, so they sit outside the physics ranking. Reported results: GenCast beat the ECMWF ensemble on 97.2% of verification targets, 99.8% past 36 hours. AIFS now posts lower error than physics models on upper-air fields. Physics models still lead on heavy precipitation scores. All three claims need their own caveats, which the sources carry.

Method

04 How to read this

  • Anomaly correlation above 0.8 counts as skillful. Below 0.6 a model has no useful skill left.
  • Scores move with the season and with events. A bad month, like the debated 2025 dip, does not rewrite the ranking alone.
  • One variable never tells the whole story. Rain, wind, and upper air each crown different winners.
  • Sources below. If a number here goes stale, the source is where the fresh one lives.

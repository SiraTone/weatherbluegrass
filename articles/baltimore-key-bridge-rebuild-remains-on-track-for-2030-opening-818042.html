Baltimore Key Bridge Rebuild Remains on Track for 2030 Opening

Newsroom Staff · August 27, 2026

BALTIMORE — Maryland transportation officials confirmed that the rebuild of the Francis Scott Key Bridge is moving forward on schedule, with no active delays on the current timeline.

During a site tour along the Patapsco River, engineers from the Maryland Transportation Authority (MDTA) provided key updates on the project's progress, budget, and upcoming contracting phases.

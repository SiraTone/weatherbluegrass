Kentucky Police Officer Arrested After Misusing Flock License Plate System to Track Ex Over 2,000 Times

Newsroom Staff · August 27, 2026

SHIVELY, Ky. — A Kentucky police officer was arrested and suspended without pay after authorities discovered he used the department’s Flock Safety automated license plate reader (ALPR) system to stalk his ex-girlfriend more than 2,000 times over a five-month span.

Officer Asad Zahir, 34, was taken into custody by the Shively Police Department following an internal investigation that revealed 2,048 unauthorized vehicle lookups between January and May.

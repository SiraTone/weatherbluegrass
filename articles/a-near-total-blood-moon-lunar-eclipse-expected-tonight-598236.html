A near-total “blood moon” lunar eclipse expected tonight

A near-total “blood moon” lunar eclipse expected tonight · August 27, 2026

The event will obscure roughly 96% of the Moon's surface within Earth's inner shadow (the umbra). While technically falling just short of a total eclipse, the vast majority of the lunar face will take on the characteristic "Blood Moon" orange-red glow. The coloration is caused by sunlight bending through Earth's atmosphere, which filters out blue light and projects the planet's sunsets and sunrises onto the lunar surface.

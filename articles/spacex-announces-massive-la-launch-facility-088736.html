SpaceX Announces Massive LA Launch Facility

Newsroom Staff · August 27, 2026

PECAN ISLAND, LA — SpaceX has officially announced plans for its fourth and largest spaceport, "Starbase Louisiana," committing $100 billion to construct a massive manufacturing and launch hub in Vermilion Parish along the state's Gulf Coast. The site will be significantly larger than SpaceX’s Starbase facility in Texas, aiming to support thousands of launches per year to handle upcoming Starship missions and orbital AI compute network deployments.

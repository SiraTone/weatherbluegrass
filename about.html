About

A weather company with a robot on the desk

CellScope builds weather tools. FRED is the face of them: an on-air robot anchor who turns official data into something you can follow.

CellScope is working on the innovation of automated weather forecasting. FRED is our first public result: a live anchor that covers severe weather, answers viewer questions, and makes videos and Shorts.

We work from National Weather Service and Storm Prediction Center data and keep legibility first. A viewer should get the hazard and the number in one glance.

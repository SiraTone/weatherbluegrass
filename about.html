About

Weather visuals, made to read

CellScope is a weather product company. We turn radar and model data into graphics that hold up on a broadcast monitor and still make sense in a timeline.

Five products are in development: 3D Storm Visualization, 3D Weather Graphs for broadcast, the CS1.0 weather AI model, the Model Visualization Suite, and the CellScope Radar app coming first to Windows.

Everything we publish goes out with the same formatting: location, date range, and peak value on the frame. Renders generate in CellScope Studio rather than being drawn by hand, so the same input always produces the same output.

What we care about

Legibility first. A viewer should get the hazard and the number in one glance, without a legend hunt, on whatever screen they happen to be watching.

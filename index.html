Why we build it this way

Graphics that carry the information

A broadcast graphic has about four seconds to make a point. So every CellScope render leads with the number that matters and puts the rest underneath.

  • 16:9 for broadcast, vertical cuts for social
  • Location, date range, and peak on every frame
  • Color scales that stay readable on a bad monitor

Before and during the storm

If you are told to evacuate

  • Go early, in daylight. Bridges and causeways close when winds reach tropical storm force.
  • Take medicine, papers, phone chargers, pets and cash.
  • Storm surge is the greatest threat to life along the coast. Do not wait to see it.

If you stay

  • Only stay if you are outside every evacuation and storm surge zone. Surge zones are not safe to ride out, even on an upper floor.
  • Shelter in an interior room, away from windows, on the lowest floor that will not flood.
  • Fill your phone, power banks and car. Store water for several days.
  • Keep generators outside, far from windows. Carbon monoxide kills after storms.

After it passes

  • Never drive or walk through floodwater.
  • Stay away from downed lines. Assume they are live.
  • The eye is not the end. Winds return from the other side.
